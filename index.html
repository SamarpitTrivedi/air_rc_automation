How it handles protected workbooks

For Office-encrypted workbooks, the page attempts browser-side decryption after you enter the password. No workbook content or password is intentionally uploaded. Success depends on browser library compatibility and the encryption format. Test with copies of representative files before business use.

Password-protected workbook

The app will attempt to decrypt this workbook locally in your browser. The password and workbook contents are not sent to a server.

If the encryption format is unsupported or the password is incorrect, the app will show an error. In that case, open the file in Excel and save an unencrypted working copy if your organization's policy permits it.
